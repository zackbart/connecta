`planningCenter()` accepts `excludeApps`, a list of Planning Center products the connection never reaches, such as `["giving"]` for a deployment that keeps agents away from donor and financial data. An excluded product's named tools leave the catalog; the description, usage guide, and version pins stop naming it; and `pco_api_get` and `pco_api_mutate` refuse its paths with `provider_permission_denied` before sending, checked against the exact URL the transport would fetch. While any product is excluded, a hatch path must be literal (no dot segments, empty segments, backslashes, whitespace, or percent-encoded delimiters), and the connection also removes the People list tools and refuses People lists, list results, rules, and conditions by path or `include`, since a list's conditions can draw on any product; refuses webhook event and delivery payloads and any change to an existing webhook subscription; and accepts a new subscription only to a reachable product's event. Any product except People can be excluded; an unknown name or People throws at construction. The default excludes nothing and is unchanged.
