Pools are the access model (decision 0006). A new top-level `maintainers` list names the people who reach plain `/mcp`, which serves the whole catalog with the deployment `readOnly`, and the operator routes that administer the deployment (catalog, activity, shared connector auth, access tokens); it is required whenever an inbound provider other than the built-in agent-token adapter is configured. Each pool now requires a non-empty static `members` list and serves exactly its `tools` to those members, with no intersection against anything else. A reference is a bare principal id resolved in the namespace of the deployment's one interactive provider, `{ namespace, id }`, or `"agent:<name>"` for a declared agent; ambiguous, unknown, repeated, or misplaced references (an agent in `maintainers`) fail construction. A non-maintainer at `/mcp`, a non-member, and an undeclared pool get the identical 404 after auth, logged as `not_maintainer`, `not_pool_member`, or `pool_not_declared`; result handles stay bound to their endpoint and paging re-admits the caller. A pool member may connect their own personal connector in that pool through `/connect/<id>`, which starts directly for non-maintainers. Shared connector auth (issuing a link, starting, completing the callback, disconnecting, and writing a credential) requires a maintainer. Breaking: `identity.connectorAccess` (with its guarded `{ tool, requireReadOnly: true }` grants; use a `readOnly: true` pool), the top-level `grant` (use `maintainers`), and pool `grant` (use `members`) are removed and fail construction naming their replacement; `describeConfig()` drops `hasGrant` at the root and on pools and `identity.connectorAccess`, and adds `maintainers` and pool `members`; anyone neither a maintainer nor a pool member gets the private 404 on operator routes, and the member access the management and operator contract entries describe is all a member gets there. Agent members reach their pools at `/agent/<pool>` (agent endpoints entry) and are refused at plain `/mcp`; `connecta doctor --pool <name>` checks a pool, and the Node template (`CONNECTA_MAINTAINERS`, a `local-machine` pool for the `local-machine` agent, `npm run doctor`) and Worker example (`CONNECTA_MAINTAINERS`, a `ci` pool for the `ci` agent) are updated accordingly.
