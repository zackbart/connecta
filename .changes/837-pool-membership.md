Pools are the access model (decision 0006). A new top-level `maintainers` list names the people who reach plain `/mcp`, which serves the whole catalog with the deployment `readOnly`, and every operator route (catalog, config, activity, connector auth, access tokens); it is required whenever an inbound provider other than the built-in agent-token adapter is configured. Each pool now requires a non-empty static `members` list and serves exactly its `tools` to those members, with no intersection against anything else. A reference is a bare principal id resolved in the namespace of the deployment's one interactive provider, `{ namespace, id }`, or `"agent:<name>"` for a declared agent; ambiguous, unknown, repeated, or misplaced references (an agent in `maintainers`) fail construction. A non-maintainer at `/mcp`, a non-member, and an undeclared pool get the identical 404 after auth, logged as `not_maintainer`, `agent_not_maintainer`, `not_pool_member`, or `pool_not_declared`; result handles stay bound to their endpoint and paging re-admits the caller. A pool member may connect their own personal connector in that pool through `/connect/<id>`, which starts directly for non-maintainers. Breaking: `identity.connectorAccess` (with its guarded `{ tool, requireReadOnly: true }` grants; use a `readOnly: true` pool), the top-level `grant` (use `maintainers`), and pool `grant` (use `members`) are removed and fail construction naming their replacement; `describeConfig()` drops `hasGrant` at the root and on pools and `identity.connectorAccess`, and adds `maintainers` and pool `members`; the operator UI and its routes serve maintainers only until the member view ships, so other people get the private 404 there. Interim until agent endpoints ship: an agent member reaches `/mcp/<pool>` with its `cta_` token and is refused at plain `/mcp`; `connecta doctor --pool <name>` checks a pool, and the Node template (`CONNECTA_MAINTAINERS`, a `local` pool for `local-machine`, `npm run doctor`) and Worker example (`CONNECTA_MAINTAINERS`, a `ci` pool) are updated accordingly.
