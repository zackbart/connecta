A direct read over its inline cap (24,000 bytes unless a connector overrides it) on a surface with `execute_code` can now answer with a sample instead of its first bytes: for rows, the row count, keys and whole first rows as valid JSON in the result's own shape; for text, the line count, head and tail. Its notice says which rows or bytes it holds and that the rest are not shown, and carries `resultId`, the original `totalBytes`, a `nextAction` program over the retained result, and the fallback when that program is refused. A program's value and the paged handle are unchanged. Results at or under the cap return whole, as before. Migration: a client that parses a truncated direct result should not assume the body after the notice is a prefix of the result: `nextOffset` is 0 and the body is a separate view, showing less than first bytes did. Writes, including a source write a deployment overrode to read, value mode, several or non-text blocks, native pools with `executeCode: false`, results with numbers `JSON.parse` would change, and any shape the rule does not cover keep their first bytes. A source write overridden to read now also says its write already ran and is no longer told to repeat the read inside a program. A `fields` miss warning is shorter: `fields/0 matched nothing after filtering, at segment 1; declared there: id, name.`, with at most 160 characters of names, and a later miss at the same place says `same as fields/0`.
