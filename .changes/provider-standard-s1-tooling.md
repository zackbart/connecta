Provider tooling now implements decision 0011's S.1. `providers:generate` writes `drift.json` version 2's `generated` block (operations, hosted names, evidence digests) and a guide's generated `## Conventions`, and refuses a provider past step 1 that breaks the `drift.json` rules or one past step 2 that breaks the guide rules. `defineProvider` accepts `blocks: { keys, select }` for a guide's `<!-- when: <key> -->` sections, which `ProviderContext.usageGuide` selects per connection, and `kit` facts for the provider kit to fill; `ProviderGuideBlocks` and `ProviderKitFacts` are exported types. `providers:check` reads version 1 and 2 records and reports manual checks last verified more than 180 days ago. `providers:spec` reads `evidence/openapi.json` (and vendored `evidence/openapi.document.json`), writes `evidence/value-safety.json`, and reads value-safety absences from the table's `absent` field in `safety.ts`. Existing providers keep their current files until their step 1; no agent-facing output changes.
