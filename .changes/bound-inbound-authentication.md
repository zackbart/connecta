After the hard deadline plus one second of grace, move stalled work into a separate allowance of 16 abandoned operations; refuse new work while it is full, log `authentication_abandoned_saturated`, expose both counters in `/health`, and bound outstanding authorizations to 32. The Worker example sets `enable_weak_ref`, which exposes `FinalizationRegistry` before the 2025-05-05 compatibility date; without it, only settlement releases a charge.
